## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intellectual rigor, structural discipline, and nuanced sophistication of top-tier architectural discourse. Conceived for architectural professionals, academic theorists, urban researchers, and design critics, the visual identity balances the authoritative weight of an established architectural monograph with the fluid, technical precision of digital drafting environments.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Architectural Constructivism**. Key characteristics include:
- Structural framing via crisp, whisper-thin drafting lines and isometric grid motifs that echo architectural orthographic drawings.
- Deep obsidian and charcoal tectonic planes contrasted against warm alabaster white ground surfaces.
- Focused accents of architectural cerulean and teal inspired by the faceted isometric geometry of the brand emblem.
- High-contrast, literary headline treatments balanced by hyper-legible, geometric sans-serif metadata.
- Frosted glassmorphism overlays that evoke vellum tracing paper and translucent structural polycarbonate panels.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the architectural studio: tectonic inks, tracing vellum, and technical blueprint pigmentation.

- **Primary (`#0B4F6C`)**: Deep architectural teal derived from the deepest facet of the brand mark. It anchors key interactive components, major section headers, and high-priority publication markers.
- **Secondary (`#1B8CA8`)**: Luminous cerulean from the mid-tone isometric facets of the brand mark, providing energetic focus states, active filter indicators, and subtle link highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26`)**: Architectural ochre / raw brass. Reserved exclusively for critical editorial flags, curatorial awards, and featured monograph callouts.
- **Neutral (`#121417`)**: Dense obsidian/charcoal ink. Used for body text, primary navigational anchors, and contrast framing in place of generic jet-black.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-alabaster` (`#FAFAFA`): The warm, non-glare foundation reminiscent of heavy archival museum board.
  - `surface-vellum` (`#F3F4F6`): Secondary structural containers and input backgrounds.
  - `border-drafting` (`rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.08)`): Ultra-fine hairline border color simulating 0.18mm technical pen rulings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between the contemplative, scholarly pace of architectural critique and the rapid precision of technical specifications.

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Expresses traditional editorial pedigree, architectural theory, and intellectual gravitas. Features optical size optimization for print-grade clarity on screens. Employs genuine italic forms for qualifying subheadings, manifesto statements, and thematic intros.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp geometric clarity, optical neutrality, and effortless scannability across dense lists, submission forms, job boards, and metadata stamps.
- **Letter Spacing**: Extended tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) is strictly reserved for uppercase category markers, dates, and isometric technical tags, creating an orthographic blueprint cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular **12-column architectural grid** framed by visible or implied alignment axes.

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1440px. Section margins extend generously to `3rem` (or `5rem` for hero showcases) to evoke wide exhibition margins and architectural portfolio mats.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Content groups (such as call-for-submission forms and monographs) collapse into balanced 4:4 splits.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Layout stacks vertically while maintaining continuous baseline rhythm and edge-to-edge section dividers.
- **Drafting Guides**: Key sections are articulated with subtle `1px` continuous horizontal and vertical hairline dividers, reinforcing tectonic discipline across multi-column article grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of tectonic material layering and architectural transparency:

- **Vellum Glassmorphism**: Floating panels, navigation bars, and sticky submission drawers utilize high-opacity backdrop filtering (`rgba(250, 250, 250, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a `1px` border of `rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.08)`). In dark mode sections, this inverts to `rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.82)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders.
- **Orthographic Tonal Tiers**: Depth is conveyed through precise shifts in tonal ground surfaces rather than blur:
  - Base: `#FAFAFA` (Ground canvas)
  - Tier 1: `#FFFFFF` (Resting content cards)
  - Tier 2: `#F3F4F6` (Recessed interactive fields, code/quote blocks)
- **Architectural Ambient Shadow**: When physical lift is needed (modal windows, floating active actions), elevation is rendered with a quiet, dual-stop dispersed shadow tinted with obsidian ink: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

In strict alignment with modern architectural geometry, structural drawings, and planar facades, the roundedness value is set to **`0` (Sharp)**.

- All containers, media viewports, badges, input controls, and buttons utilize crisp 90-degree orthogonal corners.
- Precision chamfers (subtle 45-degree corner clippings) may optionally be employed as accents on technical category chips and the hero submission badge, echoing the isometric geometry of the brand identity.
- Structural borders are strictly 1px solid hairline rules, maintaining continuous linework throughout cards and tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Crisp obsidian (`#121417`) or primary teal (`#0B4F6C`) background with alabaster white text. 0px corner radius, `0.75rem 1.75rem` padding, tracked uppercase label (`12px`, weight 600). Hover state triggers a subtle background color shift paired with an animated 3px rightward translation of an accompanying orthographic arrow (`→`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white or transparent background with a 1px drafting border (`rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.2)`). Hover fills the background with soft vellum (`#F3F4F6`) and darkens the border to `#121417`.
- **Axonometric Icon Action**: Square aspect ratio (44x44px), hairline stroke border, holding geometric glyphs with minimal hover lift.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- High-contrast, sharp badges rendered with 1px border rules.
- Uppercase `technical-mono` typography with `0.12em` letter spacing.
- Filter chips toggle between inactive (transparent background with faint border) and active (filled primary teal with crisp white text).

### Editorial & Article Cards
- Framed by 1px hairline rules on all or selective tectonic borders (e.g., strong top rule with open sides).
- Images feature high-contrast aspect ratios (16:10 or 4:5 for monographs) with a slight monochromatic hover desaturation or zoom (`1.02x` over `400ms` cubic-bezier).
- Headings set in Newsreader paired with Plus Jakarta Sans author and date stamps.

### Input Fields & Submission Forms
- Clean, architectural line inputs. Background in `#FFFFFF` or `#F3F4F6` with a defined bottom or full hairline boundary (`rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.15)`).
- On focus, border transitions directly to `#0B4F6C` with zero soft focus rings—pure 1px crisp geometry.
- Floating labels using `label-md` uppercase styling.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square checkboxes (`18x18px`) with 0px corner radius. Checked state fills with `#0B4F6C` and displays a crisp, geometric white tick mark.
- Radios feature concentric squares or precision diamonds rather than organic circles, reinforcing the axonometric architectural motif.

### Publication Monographs & Job Board Modules
- **Monograph Showcases**: Deep obsidian frames with isometric depth cues, displaying book spines and cover art with editorial citation details.
- **Job Board Items**: Clean tabular layout segmented by horizontal drafting lines, presenting title, firm name, typology, and geographic location in precise geometric columns.
